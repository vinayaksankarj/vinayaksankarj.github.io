Case studies

Three pieces of work, in short. Employer, customer, and partner names are withheld for confidentiality.

Closing the adminless groups gapA long-standing enterprise problem, solved without trading away security.

The problem

Groups could end up without an administrator. The gap had been there since the product began, and its most critical enterprise customers kept raising it.

The constraint

Customers choose the product for security and compliance. Any fix had to hold that line.

The outcome

The gap is closed, with no compromise to security.

Building an apps ecosystem on secure foundationsFrom a customer survey to an SDK, a developer portal, and paying teams.

The problem

Enterprises wanted to extend the messenger with their own apps and integrations without weakening its security model.

What we did

  • Surveyed customers. 70% of their developers wanted TypeScript/JavaScript or Java/Kotlin, which set the SDK roadmap and led to both SDKs.
  • Designed the developer documentation portal. I defined every flow and the document structure.
  • Shipped External Apps so developers can share apps across teams, and Apps Management so admins stay in control.

The outcome

10 new paying teams, 5 supported apps, new partnerships, and a growing community of developers who want to build on the messenger.

Recording and transcription, as an opt-in add-onA working AI prototype that leaves the core product’s security stance alone.

The problem

Some customers want recording and transcription. Many security-conscious ones do not want their data shared with outside AI services.

What we did

Built a working prototype that connects external AI, and positioned it as an optional add-on, not a core feature. It is marketed only to customers who are comfortable with that data sharing. The developer documentation shows how apps can connect external systems to the messenger.

The outcome

A way to offer AI features to the customers who want them, while the core product keeps its security promise for everyone else.
